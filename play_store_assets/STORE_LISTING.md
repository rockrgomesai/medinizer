Over 40% of adults aged 50+ and millions of family caregivers manage 3 to 10 daily prescriptions. Missed doses, mixing up look-alike pills, and the terror of accidental double-dosing are constant, high-stress risks.

**MedVault (SnapPill)** is a privacy-first, visual medication organizer & safety assistant designed from the ground up to keep you and your loved ones safe. 

Unlike bloated pharmaceutical apps filled with advertisements and intrusive cloud registrations, MedVault works **100% offline**, safeguarding your medical records with total confidentiality.

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES

#### 🛡️ 1. "Did I Take It?" Double-Check & Accidental Double-Dose Protection
- **Instant Glance Dashboard:** Always know if morning, afternoon, or evening pills were confirmed.
- **Accidental Double-Dose Interlock:** Tried to take a pill twice? MedVault immediately halts logging within the safety window (4 hours) with an acoustic alert and an emergency amber warning, detailing the exact time elapsed to prevent dangerous drug toxicity or hypotension.

#### 📸 2. Visual Pill Verification & Label Scanner
- **See Before You Swallow:** Photograph your actual pills and prescription bottles. When it's time for your dose, MedVault displays high-resolution photos and verified imprint badges (e.g. "L 10", "M 500").
- **On-Device Label OCR:** Point your camera at a pharmacy bottle to scan prescription details and instructions directly into your schedule without typing.

#### 📄 3. Doctor-Ready 1-Page Clinical PDF Export
- Hand your physician a clean, hospital-grade 1-page summary during appointments.
- Details active medications, exact dosages, indications, 30-day adherence percentage, drug allergies, emergency contacts, and a doctor signature line.
- Print directly or send via email in one tap.

#### 👨‍👩‍👧 4. Caregiver Link & Missed-Dose SOS
- Adult children and family caregivers can stay connected. If a critical morning blood pressure or insulin dose is unconfirmed, generate an instant pre-formatted SMS alert or phone call.

#### 💊 5. Prescription Refill Tracker
- Live inventory tracking decrements automatically as you take doses.
- Visual alerts notify you when supplies fall below 7 days.
- Call your pharmacy's refill line in a single tap.

#### 👁️ 6. Senior Mode & Spoken Voice Assistant
- One-tap switch to extra-large touch targets (22sp+ high-contrast typography).
- Spoken dosage announcements powered by Android Text-To-Speech read instructions aloud for low-vision seniors.
