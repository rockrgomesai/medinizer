Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

MedVault ("the App") is committed to total privacy and healthcare confidentiality. We believe medical adherence tools should protect lives without collecting or monetizing personal health data.

1. Zero Cloud Data Collection

MedVault does not transmit, store, or process your medical information on remote servers. All medication schedules, pill photos, prescription labels, adherence timestamps, and emergency contact details reside exclusively in your device's secure local sandbox storage.

2. Permissions Used & Clinical Purpose

  • Camera: Used exclusively on-device to photograph pills and scan prescription bottle labels via ML Kit. No photos are uploaded or transmitted externally.
  • Notifications: Used strictly to display local, scheduled medication reminder notifications.

3. Zero Advertising & Third-Party Trackers

MedVault contains no third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or pharmaceutical marketing brokers.

4. Contact & Compliance

For questions or verification regarding this policy, please reach us at privacy@medvaulthealth.app.
